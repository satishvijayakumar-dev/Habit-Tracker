# ActivHealth v2.0 — User Journey Map

**Design principle:** one coach-led thread from first open to week one. The user never has to figure out the app — the coach tells them the next right thing, the ring shows progress, and everything else stays out of the way.

---

## The journey

### Minute 0 — First open (Welcome)
- Dark brand moment: gradient logo, "ActivHealth — Small loops. Real momentum."
- One question: **"What should your coach call you?"** — this powers every coach message from here on.
- One CTA: "Find my path". No account, no friction, no permissions asked yet.

### Minutes 1–2 — Path quiz (4 questions)
- Animated progress bar; one tap per question with haptic confirmation.
- Questions cover lifestyle, desired outcome, what breaks routines, and food basics — answers score into one of 7 personas (Gym Builder, Runner/Walker, Social Sports, Office, Remote, Starter, Balanced).
- Escape hatch for the impatient: "Skip — I'll guide myself."

### Minute 2 — Persona reveal (the magic moment)
- The persona card scales in with a colored glow: "**Sam, your path is The Gym Builder**".
- Coach offers 3 starter loops, pre-designed with anchor/fallback/celebration — or build your own.
- Outcome: the user lands on Today with loops already in place and a reason to come back tomorrow.

### Day 1 — Today tab (the home)
- "Morning, Sam." + persona chip + the **Momentum Ring**: each loop owns an equal segment; the inner mint arc tracks weekly active minutes vs their personal target.
- Loops are **1-tap check-ins** (down from 4 taps in v1.5). On completion: haptic + the user's own celebration phrase ("That counts") — completed loops tint mint and sink to the bottom, never struck through.
- One coach nudge max ("Quick check-in with your coach — 10 seconds").
- One session card ("Today's session → Start").
- Floating "+" logs any activity in ~3 interactions.

### Day 1–2 — Coach tab
- Pulsing coral orb identity, tuned to path + goal.
- 10-second daily check-in (Energy / Soreness / Time / Mood) — **persisted now**, so the coach remembers, and mood actually changes the advice.
- The coach reply types in, addressing the user by name with real data ("Sam, low battery noted. Your minimum effective dose is enough…").
- One primary CTA: Start session. Week shown as a 7-dot strip. Nutrition is a "Fuel" topic inside Coach, not a competing tab.

### Day 3+ — Celebration ladder
- Streak milestones at 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 / 60 / 100 days trigger a full-screen moment (glow card + heavy haptic + "Keep it going").
- Closing all loops puts the ring in its glowing "Closed" state.
- Logging a session pops back to Today so the ring visibly advances — effort → progress, made visceral.

### Week 1 — You tab
- "Your week" gradient bar chart, count-up stat tiles, per-loop 14-day heat strips, BMI/body card.
- This is where the user sees their identity forming: "I'm someone with a 9-day streak."

### Ongoing — Community tab
- Social-sport groups (Pickleball, Badminton, Tennis, Padel, Squash, 5-a-side…), skill bands, privacy ladder (invite-only default).
- Honest single locked card: "Live matching arrives with accounts." No fake members anywhere.

### Monetisation touchpoints (neat, never nagging)
- Free = 3 active loops + full coach + logging. The cap is generous enough to form the habit.
- Adding a 4th loop opens the **Pro paywall** (the natural "I'm invested" moment).
- A single Pro card sits on the You tab. £3.99/mo or £24.99/yr (founder pricing); during beta, Pro unlocks free so the whole journey is testable.
- Pro identity: "One small subscription keeps ActivHealth independent — no ads, no selling your health data."

---

## Journey rules (for any future feature)

1. Nothing lands on Today unless it serves *today*. Everything else goes to Coach, Community, or You.
2. Every stat appears in exactly one home (ring = today, You = trends). No duplication.
3. Every completion gets feedback (haptic + phrase); every milestone gets a moment.
4. The coach speaks with one warm voice, uses the user's name, and never claims abilities it doesn't have.
5. One primary CTA per screen.
