1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uses the ActivHealth App. ActivHealth is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16; if you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

2. The data we collect

ActivHealth is local-first — much of your data lives only on your device. We collect the following:

Account data (only if you sign in)

  • Email address, and your name and Apple/Google account identifier when you sign in with Apple or Google.
  • A user identifier we generate for you.

Profile & health-and-fitness data

  • Age, sex, height, weight and weight history, fitness goal, and activity level.
  • Your habits, activities, workouts, calories, check-ins, streaks, and progress.
  • This includes health and wellness information, treated as special-category data (see section 4).

Community data (only if you opt in)

  • A display name you choose, the groups you create or join, and the messages you send in group chats.

Location data (only if you enable community discovery)

  • Your device location is used on your device to find nearby groups. Only a coarse, approximate area (roughly 1 km) — never your precise location — is ever stored or shared, and only when you opt in.

AI Coach conversations

  • When you message the in-app AI Coach, your message and minimal context (e.g. your persona and goal) are sent to our AI provider to generate a reply (see section 5).

Technical & diagnostic data

  • If enabled, anonymous crash and error reports (device model, OS version, error details) to help us fix bugs. These do not include your health data or message content.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Health and wellness data

Some data you enter (weight, activity, and related metrics) may be considered special-category health data. We process it only to provide the App’s features to you, on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give when you enter this data and use these features. 6. How long we keep your data

  • On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • Account and community data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we permanently delete your account and associated community data from our backend, and wipe your data from the device. Backups and logs are purged on our providers’ normal rolling cycle.
  • Diagnostic/crash data is retained on a short rolling basis and is not linked to your health data.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and to withdraw consent. You also have the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects — the AI Coach provides guidance only and makes no such decisions.

  • Delete everything: use Settings → Delete account in the App at any time.
  • Other requests: email [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Our backend enforces per-user access controls (row-level security) so users can only access their own data and the community content they’re entitled to. Your device data is protected by your device passcode/biometrics. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Not medical advice

ActivHealth and its AI Coach provide general fitness and wellbeing guidance only. They are not a medical device and do not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified professional before starting any exercise or nutrition programme, and stop and seek help if you feel unwell.
